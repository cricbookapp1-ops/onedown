OneDown — Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This covers two things that physically move: equipment you buy from the shop, and equipment we collect from you for repair.

Shop orders

Where we deliver

Anywhere in India that our courier partners reach. We do not ship internationally.

If your PIN code turns out to be unserviceable after you order, we will contact you and refund in full rather than leave the order sitting.

What delivery costs
Standard delivery ₹49 flat
Free delivery on orders of ₹1,499 and above

The exact figures are shown on the checkout screen before you pay, and they are read from the platform settings at that moment — so what you are quoted is what you are charged.

How long it takes

  • Dispatch: within 1–2 working days of payment being confirmed. Cash-on delivery orders are dispatched from the moment they are placed.
  • In transit: typically 3–7 working days depending on distance. Metros are usually at the faster end.

These are working days — Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Festival periods and weather disruption can add to them, and we will tell you if your parcel is affected.

Tracking

Every order has a tracker in My orders showing where it is: placed → packed → shipped → delivered. When it ships you get the courier's name and tracking reference, and a notification on your phone.

If nobody is home

Couriers usually attempt delivery up to three times. After that the parcel comes back to us, and we will contact you to arrange a re-send or a refund. A re-send after three failed attempts may carry the delivery charge again.

Damaged in transit

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph it before opening it if you can, and tell us within 7 days. We cover the return and send a replacement or refund you in full. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Repair pickups and returns

Collection

Once you have accepted a quote and paid, we arrange collection from the address on the request. We confirm the pickup window with you first — we will not send somebody unannounced.

Collection is usually within 2–3 working days of payment in serviceable areas.

Turnaround

The quote tells you the expected turnaround in working days, counted from the day your item reaches the workshop — not from the day you paid. Most bat repairs are 5–7 working days in the workshop.

You can follow it in the App: paid → pickup scheduled → picked up → repairing → repaired → out for delivery → delivered.

Return

We return the item to the same address it was collected from, at no extra charge. If you need it sent somewhere else, tell us before it ships.

Packing your item

Please wrap the item so it survives the journey to us — bubble wrap or a bat sleeve is enough. We are not able to compensate for damage caused in transit to us by inadequate packing, though we will always tell you honestly if we think that is what happened.

Address changes

An order and a repair each keep a copy of the address they were sent to, taken at the moment you placed them. Editing your saved address book later will not redirect something already on its way — that is deliberate, so a parcel cannot silently change destination mid-journey.

If you need to change the destination of something already placed, contact us before it ships and we will do what we can.
