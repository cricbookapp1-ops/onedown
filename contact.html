What to tell us

It helps enormously if you include the reference from the App:

  • A booking — the ground's name and the date and time of the slot.
  • A shop order — the order number from My orders (the short one, like #12).
  • A repair — the repair number from the request (like #7).
  • A payment problem — the amount and the date. You do not need to know anything technical; we can find it from that.
