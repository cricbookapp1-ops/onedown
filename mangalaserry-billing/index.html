OneDown Billing

A simple billing app for restaurants, hotels and tea shops. Take orders, print receipts on a Bluetooth printer and see daily sales reports. It works offline, and all data stays on the phone. If you want, you can turn on backup to your own Google Drive so your bills are safe if the phone is lost or the app is removed.

Features

  • Your own restaurant name, logo, colours and receipt design
  • Fast billing with your menu, cash / UPI / card, tax and discounts
  • Bluetooth receipt printers (58 mm and 80 mm)
  • Daily and monthly sales reports
  • Optional automatic backup to your own Google Drive, and backup files you can save anywhere
