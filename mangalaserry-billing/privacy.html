Privacy Policy – OneDown Billing

Last updated: 13 November 2026

OneDown Billing ("the app") is a billing app for restaurants. This policy explains what happens to information in the app. We, the developers, do not run any server for the app, and we never receive your bills or any other data from it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Bills, menu items, prices, restaurant details, settings and sales reports you create are saved on your phone, inside the app's own storage. They leave the device only in the two ways you choose yourself: a backup file you save or share using Android's share menu, or the optional Google Drive backup described below.

Optional Google Drive backup (Google sign-in)

You can choose to sign in with your Google account in Settings → Backup & restore. This is optional and the app works fully without it. If you sign in:

  • What the app accesses: your Google account's email address (to show which account the backups go to), and the app's own private "app data" folder in your Google Drive (Google permission drive.appdata). The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • How it is used: the app saves copies of its own data (bills, menu, restaurant details and settings) to that private folder, automatically after billing and when the app is closed, so you can restore them on a new phone or after reinstalling. When you restore, the app reads those copies back.
  • Where it is stored: only in your own Google Drive account, sent directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection. The backups are not sent to us or to anyone else. Each phone keeps its most recent 30 days of backups; older ones are removed by the app.
  • Sharing: we do not share, sell or transfer this data, and we do not use it for advertising or any other purpose.

OneDown Billing's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Bluetooth and location permission

The app uses Bluetooth only to find and connect to a receipt printer and send bills to it. On Android 11 and older, Android requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning; the app does not read or store your location.

Internet

The app uses the internet only for the optional Google sign-in and Google Drive backup. Without Google backup turned on, it does not send your data over the internet.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all data on the phone.
  • Signing out in Settings → Backup & restore stops the backup and removes the app's access to your Google account. Backups already saved stay in your Drive so you can restore them later.
  • To delete the Google Drive backups, open Google Drive on a computer → Settings → Manage apps → OneDown Billing → Options → Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
