Privacy Policy – Mangalaserry Billing

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Mangalaserry Billing ("the app") is a billing app for Mangalaserry Restaurant, Kariyad, Kerala, India. This policy explains what happens to information in the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Bills, menu items, prices and sales reports you create are saved only on your phone, inside the app's own storage. They never leave the device unless you choose to export a backup file, which you save or share yourself using Android's share menu.

Bluetooth and location permission

The app uses Bluetooth only to find and connect to a receipt printer and send bills to it. On Android 11 and older, Android requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning; the app does not read or store your location.

Internet

The app does not send your data over the internet.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all data it holds.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
