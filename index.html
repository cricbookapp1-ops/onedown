How to book and pay

Bookings, tournament entries, shop orders and repairs are made in the OneDown Android app. Customers sign in with their mobile number and password, choose what they want, and pay through Razorpay Checkout inside the app (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets). This website has no separate login; it holds our policies and contact details.

  1. Sign in to the OneDown app with your mobile number and password.
  2. Pick a date and a free match slot, a tournament, a product, or accept a repair quote.
  3. Review the amount shown (for a ground slot, a ₹500 advance per team).
  4. Pay through Razorpay. Your booking, entry, order or repair is confirmed as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card or UPI details.

A booking, tournament entry, order or repair is confirmed only once Razorpay tells our servers the payment succeeded — so what you see in the app is the state your money is actually in. Refunds go back to the method they came from.

All prices are in Indian Rupees and include GST where applicable. See the cancellation & refund policy for what happens when plans change.
