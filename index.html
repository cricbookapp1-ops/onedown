How to book and pay

Everything is done in the ACG Android app (run by OneDown). Customers sign in with their mobile number and password. Only two things are paid online, through Razorpay Checkout inside the app (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets): our ground-booking fee and repair charges. This website has no separate login; it holds our policies and contact details.

  1. Sign in to the ACG app with your mobile number and password.
  2. Pick a date and a free match slot, or accept a repair quote.
  3. Review the amount shown (for a ground slot, our ₹139 booking fee per team).
  4. Pay through Razorpay. Your booking or repair is confirmed as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment.
  5. Pay the ground's fee to the ground owner at the ground.

Payments

Online payments (our booking fee and repair charges) are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card or UPI details. We collect only our own money online: the ground's fee goes to the ground owner directly, at the ground.

A booking or repair is confirmed only once Razorpay tells our servers the payment succeeded — so what you see in the app is the state your money is actually in. Refunds go back to the method they came from.

All prices are in Indian Rupees. We are not registered for GST, so no GST is charged. See the cancellation & refund policy for what happens when plans change.
