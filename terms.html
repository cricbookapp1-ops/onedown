OneDown — Terms of Service

Last updated: 17 September 2026

By creating an account or using OneDown ("the App"), you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. What OneDown is

OneDown does three things, and it is worth being clear which of them we are a party to:

  1. Ground booking — we are the introducer, not the venue. Grounds are listed, owned and run by third parties. We show you their availability and pass your booking to them. The pitch, its condition, its safety and whether it is open when you arrive are the ground's responsibility, not ours.
  2. The shop — we are the seller. Equipment listed in the shop is sold by us. Your contract for those goods is with us.
  3. The repair centre — we are the repairer. We collect, repair and return your equipment ourselves.

2. Your account

You need an account to book, buy or send anything for repair. You must give a real name and a working mobile number, because a ground owner has to be able to reach you and a courier has to be able to find you.

You are responsible for what happens under your account. Do not share your sign-in. Tell us promptly if you think somebody else has access to it.

You must be 18 or older, or using the App with the involvement of a parent or guardian who accepts these terms on your behalf.

3. Booking a ground

  • A slot takes up to two teams, and each team books and pays for itself. A ground may instead sell the whole slot to one team; its listing says which.
  • Choosing a slot holds it for ten minutes while you pay. Nobody else can start booking that slot while you are paying. If you do not finish in time, the slot goes back on sale.
  • You pay an advance online — shown in full before you pay — and the rest of the ground's fee at the ground on the day. The price is set by the ground.
  • The first team to confirm chooses the ball (red or white) for that slot. The second team sees it and cannot change it. A ground may change the ball afterwards, and both teams are told why.
  • Bookings cannot be cancelled by you, and the advance is not refunded if you do not play. If the ground or OneDown cancels, the whole advance comes back to you as a booking credit with that ground, valid 30 days.
  • Cancellation, credits and refunds are covered by our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
  • Turn up on time and leave the ground as you found it. A ground may refuse entry for unsafe behaviour, and we may suspend an account that repeatedly fails to show up for confirmed bookings.

4. Listing a ground

If you list a venue, you confirm that you own it or are authorised to let it, and that its details and photographs are accurate and yours to publish.

Listings are reviewed before players can see them, and an approved listing returns for review when its key details change. We may decline, suspend or remove a listing — and we will tell you why.

You are responsible for honouring bookings you take, for the condition and safety of the venue, and for any tax on what you earn.

Advances are collected by OneDown and paid over to you on a monthly settlement, less a platform commission of ₹100 for each booked slot (not per team) and any GST on it. The commission and the advance are frozen on each booking when it is made, so a later change to either never rewrites what a booking earned. If you cancel a booking, the team's advance goes back to them as a credit and is not settled to you.

5. The shop

Prices are in Indian Rupees and include GST where applicable. We do our best to keep the catalogue accurate, but if an item is listed at an obviously wrong price or is out of stock after you order, we will cancel and refund rather than ship something we cannot honour.

Stock is held when you place an order, not when you pay. An unpaid order releases its stock when its payment window lapses.

Delivery is covered by our Shipping Policy.

6. Repairs

We quote from the photographs you send. If the damage turns out to be worse than the photographs showed, we will come back to you with a revised price before doing any further work — we will not do work you have not agreed to.

Repairs are to cricket equipment. We are not liable for sentimental or collectible value beyond the item's replacement cost, and we will tell you before accepting anything we think we cannot improve.

Photographs you upload for a repair are stored privately and are visible only to you and the team assessing your request.

7. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your full card or UPI credentials.

A booking, order or repair is only confirmed when our payment provider tells our servers the payment succeeded. A message on your phone saying a payment went through is not by itself a confirmation — the App will show you the confirmed state once it has it, usually within seconds.

If you are charged and nothing is confirmed, contact us with the amount and the date. We will find it and put it right.
