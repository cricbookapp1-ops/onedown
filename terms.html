OneDown — Terms of Service

Last updated: 17 September 2026

By creating an account or using OneDown ("the App"), you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. What OneDown is

OneDown runs the ACG booking app. It does four things, and it is worth being clear which of them we are a party to:

  1. Ground booking — we provide the booking service, not the venue. The ground is owned and run by an independent ground owner. We show you its availability, hold your slot and pass your booking to the owner. The only money we collect for a booking is our own booking fee, paid online. The ground's fee is paid directly to the ground owner at the ground. The pitch, its condition, its safety and whether it is open when you arrive are the ground's responsibility, not ours.
  2. Tournaments — organised by the ground. We let you enter your team in the app. The entry fee is paid to the organiser at the ground, and the organiser confirms your team once it is paid. We do not collect entry fees.
  3. The shop — cash on delivery. Equipment is listed by us and by independent sellers; each product says who sells it. Orders are paid in cash on delivery — nothing is charged online.
  4. The repair centre — we are the repairer. We collect, repair and return your equipment ourselves, and the agreed repair price is paid to us online.

2. Your account

You need an account to book, buy or send anything for repair. You must give a real name and a working mobile number, because a ground owner has to be able to reach you and a courier has to be able to find you.

You are responsible for what happens under your account. Do not share your sign-in. Tell us promptly if you think somebody else has access to it.

You must be 18 or older, or using the App with the involvement of a parent or guardian who accepts these terms on your behalf.

3. Booking a ground

  • A slot takes up to two teams, and each team books and pays for itself. A ground may instead sell the whole slot to one team; its listing says which.
  • Choosing a slot holds it for ten minutes while you pay. Nobody else can start booking that slot while you are paying. If you do not finish in time, the slot goes back on sale.
  • You pay our booking fee online — shown in full before you pay — and the ground's fee (the rest of the slot price) to the ground owner at the ground on the day. The slot price is set by the ground.
  • The first team to confirm chooses the ball (red or white) for that slot. The second team sees it and cannot change it. A ground may change the ball afterwards, and both teams are told why.
  • Bookings cannot be cancelled by you, and the booking fee is not refunded if you do not play. If the ground or OneDown cancels, the whole booking fee comes back to you as a booking credit, valid 30 days.
  • Cancellation, credits and refunds are covered by our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
  • Turn up on time and leave the ground as you found it. A ground may refuse entry for unsafe behaviour, and we may suspend an account that repeatedly fails to show up for confirmed bookings.

4. Tournaments

  • Tournaments are organised and run by the ground. Each tournament page shows the entry fee, dates and format before you enter.
  • Entering puts your team on the list. Pay the entry fee to the organiser at the ground; the organiser marks your team in once it is paid.
  • Prizes, if any, are fixed by the organiser in advance and do not depend on how many teams enter.
  • Refunds of entry fees are the organiser's — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

5. Listing a ground

If you list a venue, you confirm that you own it or are authorised to let it, and that its details and photographs are accurate and yours to publish.

Listings are reviewed before players can see them, and an approved listing returns for review when its key details change. We may decline, suspend or remove a listing — and we will tell you why.

You are responsible for honouring bookings you take, for the condition and safety of the venue, and for any tax on what you earn.

You collect your own money. Teams pay the slot fee and any tournament entry fee to you directly at the ground. OneDown collects only its own booking fee online from each team; it never holds money that is yours, so there is nothing to settle. The booking fee is frozen on each booking when it is made. If you cancel a booking, the team's booking fee goes back to them as a credit.

6. The shop

Prices are in Indian Rupees and include GST where applicable. We do our best to keep the catalogue accurate, but if an item is listed at an obviously wrong price or is out of stock after you order, we will cancel the order rather than ship something we cannot honour.

Shop orders are cash on delivery: you pay the courier when the parcel arrives. Stock is held from the moment you place the order.

Delivery is covered by our Shipping Policy.

7. Repairs

We quote from the photographs you send. If the damage turns out to be worse than the photographs showed, we will come back to you with a revised price before doing any further work — we will not do work you have not agreed to.

Repairs are to cricket equipment. We are not liable for sentimental or collectible value beyond the item's replacement cost, and we will tell you before accepting anything we think we cannot improve.

Photographs you upload for a repair are stored privately and are visible only to you and the team assessing your request.

8. Payments

Online payments — our booking fee and repair charges — are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your full card or UPI credentials. The ground's fee, tournament entry fees and shop orders are not paid online.

A booking or repair is only confirmed when our payment provider tells our servers the payment succeeded. A message on your phone saying a payment went through is not by itself a confirmation — the App will show you the confirmed state once it has it, usually within seconds.

If you are charged and nothing is confirmed, contact us with the amount and the date. We will find it and put it right.

  • Attempt to interfere with the App, its servers, or other people's accounts and data.
  • Post abusive, misleading or unlawful content in reviews, messages or listings.
  • Scrape, resell or systematically extract listings or contact details.

We may suspend or close an account that does any of this.

Reviews must describe an actual visit. We remove reviews we believe to be fake, whether they are flattering or not.
