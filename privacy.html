OneDown — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

OneDown ("the App", "we", "us") is a cricket platform with three parts: it helps cricketers find and book cricket grounds and helps ground owners take those bookings; it sells cricket equipment; and it repairs damaged cricket gear, collecting it from and returning it to your address. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using OneDown you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

a) Account information. Your account is your mobile number and a password you choose. The number must be a ten-digit Indian mobile, and one number holds one account. We store the password only in a hashed form that cannot be read back — not even by us.

b) Profile information you provide. When you make an account we ask for your name, and your city and area, chosen from a list. A ground owner sees your name and mobile number when you book, so they can confirm the slot and reach you on the day.

c) Booking & transaction information. When you book a ground we store the ground, date, time slot, the teams you name (if any) and the amount. A booking is held with OneDown's booking fee, paid online; the ground's price is paid to the ground owner in person, and the ground records when it has been. Payments are processed by our payment provider (Razorpay); we do not store your full card or UPI credentials — those are handled by the provider under their own privacy policy.

d) Content you create. A message you send with a booking request, a review you leave for a ground, and — for the ground owner — the ground's details, photos and contact number.

e) Delivery and pickup addresses. If you order from the shop or book a repair, we store the address you enter, together with the name and mobile number on it, so a parcel can be delivered and an item collected. An order and a repair each keep a copy of the address they were sent to, so editing your address book later cannot redirect something that is already in transit. Saved addresses are visible only to you; an order or repair's copy is visible to the OneDown team fulfilling it.

f) Shop orders. What you bought, how much it cost, and the delivery address and status of the order.

g) Repair requests and their photos. What you sent for repair, your description of the damage, the quote, and the photos you upload. Repair photos are stored in a private bucket: there is no public URL for them, and they can only be opened by you and by the OneDown team assessing the request, through a short-lived signed link. Please photograph the damaged item and keep other people and personal documents out of frame.

h) Information stored only on your device. Saved grounds are kept locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

i) Technical information. Basic, non-identifying diagnostics needed to run the service (e.g. error logs). The App requests only the Internet permission.

We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, or microphone data. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a photo to your profile or, for the ground owner, to the ground.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To take and manage ground bookings, and payments where enabled.
  • To show you grounds, their availability and their prices.
  • To take, pack and deliver shop orders.
  • To assess, quote, collect, repair and return items sent to the Repair Centre.
  • To operate, secure, debug and improve the App.
  • To communicate with you about bookings, orders, repairs and account activity.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How information is shared

  • Ground owners see the bookings made on their grounds — your name, mobile number and any message you sent with the request — so they can confirm the slot and contact you. Nothing else from your profile is shared. You see a ground's contact number only while you hold a booking there.
  • Service providers that run the App on our behalf:
    • Supabase (database, authentication, hosting).
    • Razorpay (payment processing).
    • Google (Sign-In).
  • The OneDown team fulfilling a shop order sees what you ordered and the address it is going to. The team assessing a repair sees your photos, your description and the collection address. Ground owners see none of this.
  • Delivery partners are given the name, number and address needed to deliver a parcel or collect and return a repair, and nothing else.
  • Other users: a review you write shows your name publicly on that ground's page. You can delete it at any time.
  • Legal: where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal/accounting obligations. You can request deletion (see Section 6).

5. Security

Access to data is protected by authentication and database row-level security policies so users can only read/write data they are permitted to. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access / update: edit your profile in the App.
  • Delete your account & data: email saimithran0911@gmail.com and we will delete your account and associated personal data — including saved addresses, orders, repair requests and their photos — within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.
  • Remove a saved address: You → Saved addresses → Remove.
  • Sign out: removes your session from the device.

7. Children

OneDown is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: saimithran0911@gmail.com.
