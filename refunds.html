OneDown — Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

OneDown does three separate things, and each one cancels differently because each one goes wrong differently. The rules below are the ones the app actually enforces — they are not aspirations, they are what the buttons do.

1. Booking a cricket ground

A slot at a ground takes up to two teams, and each team books and pays for itself. What you pay online is our booking fee — always shown before you pay — which holds your team's place. The ground's fee (the rest of the slot price) is paid to the ground owner at the ground on the day.

You cannot cancel a booking, and the booking fee is not refunded if you decide not to play. The app says so on the checkout screen, in those words, and you have to accept it before you pay: a slot that is held for your team is a slot nobody else could book.

If the ground or OneDown cancels on you. You get the whole booking fee back as a booking credit, automatically and immediately. A credit is worth what you paid, is valid for 30 days, and is applied to your next booking before anything is charged to your card — if it covers the booking fee in full, you pay nothing at all. You are told why the booking was cancelled, in the ground's own words.

If you would rather have the money back than a credit, ask us. We can return a credit to the card or account it came from, as far as that payment can be refunded.

If your payment arrives late. A slot is held for ten minutes while you pay. If a payment reaches us after that hold has run out and the place has gone, the payment is refunded in full, automatically — we never keep money for a booking that does not exist.

The ground's fee. That money is paid to the ground owner and never passes through OneDown, so there is nothing here for us to refund. Settle it with the ground directly.

2. Shop orders

Before it ships. You can cancel from My orders at any point up to the moment the parcel is handed to the courier. Orders are cash on delivery, so nothing has been charged.

Once it has shipped. The cancel button is gone, because there is a box with your name on it moving through a courier network. Contact us — we will usually be able to help, but it becomes a conversation rather than a button.

Damaged, wrong or faulty items. Tell us within 7 days of delivery, with a photograph. We pay the return shipping and refund you in full by UPI or bank transfer, or send a replacement, whichever you prefer.

Declining a parcel. No money has reached us, so there is nothing to refund. Simply decline the parcel, or cancel before it ships.

3. Repairs

Before you accept the quote. Sending photographs and getting a price costs nothing. Decline the quote, or let it expire, and that is the end of it.

After you accept and pay, before we collect. Cancel from the request and you are refunded in full. We have not touched your equipment yet, so there is nothing to unwind.

Once we have collected your item. Cancelling is no longer a button, because we are holding your bat. Call us. If we have not started work we will return it and refund you less the collection cost; if work has begun we will tell you exactly what has been done and what that leaves.

If we cannot repair it. If we open your item and find the damage is worse than the photographs showed, we will tell you before doing anything further. If you do not want to go ahead at the revised price, we return it and refund you in full, including collection.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back the way the money came — to the original card, UPI ID, net banking account or wallet. We cannot redirect a refund to a different account; that is a limitation of the payment network, not a policy choice.

Once we issue a refund, Razorpay processes it and your bank credits it. That typically takes 5–7 working days, and occasionally up to 10 during bank holidays. We have no way to speed this up once it has left us — if it has been longer than 10 working days, contact us with your order or booking reference and we will chase it.

Partial refunds are possible and are shown as such: you will see the amount that went back and the amount that did not.
